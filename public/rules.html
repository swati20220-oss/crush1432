1. Copyright & Intellectual Property Disclaimer

RAGAA does not host, store, download, or index any copyrighted audio or media files on its own servers. All content, audio streams, thumbnails, titles, and artist data are indexed and streamed directly via official third-party APIs and authorized embed players (YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube IFrame Embed Player) in compliance with their developer policies.

2. Compliance with Information Technology (IT) Rules

This service operates as an intermediary under the guidelines of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules and applicable Indian IT laws. The platform acts solely as a user interface client to access publicly accessible media via official API protocols.

3. Non-Commercial & Educational Usage

This software project is created solely for demonstration, educational, and personal utility purposes. No subscription fees, monetization, or paywalls are implemented. All intellectual property, trademarks, song rights, and artist branding remain the exclusive property of their respective record labels and original creators.

4. Takedown & Grievance Contact

If you are a copyright owner or legal representative and believe that any embedded link violates your rights, please remove or restrict embed permissions directly at the source hosting service or contact the developer for immediate UI removal.
